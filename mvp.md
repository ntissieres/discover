<h1>Minimal Viable Product</h1>
<h2>Define the problem and the targeted audience</h2>
<h3>What is the problem I want to solve ?</h3>
I want everyone to learn progressively how to make music through a competitive video game
<h3>Ideal users</h3>
- Benjamin, a man who always wanted to make music but never did because of the time it takes to learn and the difficulty it represents
- Alice, an already validated artist that want to show her talent in sound design and gain more visibility by streaming the game
- Sarah, a beginner in music production that often have the blank page syndrome, who want to find inspiration by playing with other artists and learn new sound design
- Diego, a competitive gamer who always seeks to master the game he plays and who wants intersesting and deep gameplay over the rest
<h3>Analyze already existing product</h3>
Most of the existing video games that teach you music production are solo games, and looks more like courses that actual entertainement. They are good to learn the theory but are not good enough to be streamable on twitch.
<h2>Functionnalities to prioritize</h2>
<h3>Must have</h3>
- Being able to create an account and log in
- Being able to host a game (if it's easier than using servers to start with)
- Being able to join a game
- Having a minimal synthetizer with :
    - 3 waveforms: Sine, Saw, Square
    - a volume knob
    - a pitch knob
    - a volume enveloppe with attack, decay, sustain, release knob
- On each round of the game:
    - the player should have his inventory filled with parts of the synthetizer
    - the player should be able to use things in his inventory to create sounds
    - the player should be able to use those sounds to create a midi melody
    - Once done, the player should be able to chose from among the other players in the game which music she/he will continue, and start the next round on top of it.
- the game stops after 6 rounds and counts the number of points from each player and show the classement. Whenever another player choose your music to continue, you gain one point
<h3>Should have</h3>
- In the synthetizer:
    - a filter with only three modes (lowpass, highpass, bandpass) and a knob to choose the frequency
    - one/multiple enveloppes with attack, decay, sustain, release knob
    - one/multiple lfos with frequency knob and mode sine, saw, square
- For each round, the possibility to chose 3 actions to fill the inventory. 3 items are shown randomly. Asking to show 3 new items consume one action. Adding one item to your inventory consume one action
<h3>Could have</h3>
- In the synthetizer:
    - A simple distortion with only one knob to choose the intensity
    - A simple reverb with only one knob to choose the intensity
    - A simple delay with one knob for the intensity and another for the frequency
- Being able to join games with unknown users
- Storing the music that were made during the game
<h3>Won't have (to do for a future version)</h3>
- The possibility to keep the sound you use in the game to make your own music
- The possibility to use the synthetizer outside the game to create your own music
- The possibility to collaborate in real time with other users on the same project to create music
- Ranking according to your results in your games (elo style)
- Choosing the music genre in which you want to launch the game
- Smartphone version
<h2>User journey</h2>
Inscription -> host a game/join a game -> play the 6 rounds (5min each)
for each rounds : fill the inventory -> use elements in the inventory to create the sounds or modify sounds we have in the chosen music (after 1st round) -> create midi melody -> assemble the midi melody -> (end of the 5min) -> chose a music from another player, and go to the next round
