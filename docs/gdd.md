<h1>Game Design Document</h1>

<h2>Game Pitch</h2>

In this game, players will confront other in a battle of creativity around music. They will create their sounds and their melody with some constraints, and iteratively share their work between each other. The most inspiring player wins the game.

<h2>Target Players & Fantasy</h2>

- Feel like an artist that is inspiring for others
- Discover new way of producing music
- Feel inventive by transforming dust into a banger
- Experience the pressure of finishing the creation in time

<h2>Core Gameplay Loop</h2>

1. Players receive limited tools
2. Players create a short musical idea under time pressure
3. Players listen to each other’s creations
4. Players choose which track to continue
5. Points are awarded
6. Next round builds on the chosen track

<h2>Game Rules & Constraints</h2>

- Number of players: 4
- Round duration: 5min
- Number of rounds: 3 rounds
- How points are earned: at the end of each round, you gain one point for each player who selected your creation
- How the game ends: at the end of the third round, the player with the most points wins the game

<h2>Player Agency & Strategy</h2>

- From nothing or weird tools to banger creation
- Sound design and melody choices to be selected by other players
- Being able to do more than others within the time limit

<h2>Why This Is Fun/Unique</h2>

- Each game has its own constraints, which ensures replayability. Those constraints come from the tools that are randomly given to you and the creations from other players.

- Playing the game and seeing other creations helps you learn how to make music

- Being confronted to other player creativity helps you imagine new way of making music
